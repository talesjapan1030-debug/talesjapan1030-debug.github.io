Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Tales Japan Uploader ("the App") is a personal tool operated by Tales Japan. It publishes Tales Japan's own original short videos, Japanese folktales narrated in simple English, to the Tales Japan accounts on TikTok and YouTube.

Information the App accesses

When the account owner signs in, the App receives an access token and basic account information, such as the account's open ID and display name, from TikTok or Google. The App uses these only to upload videos to that same account.

How information is used

  • To upload videos, titles and captions created by Tales Japan to the Tales Japan account.
  • To check the status of an upload.

Information is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The App does not run a server or database, and it does not collect information about viewers or other TikTok or YouTube users.

Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share any information with third parties.

Deleting your data

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok or Google account settings. Deleting the token file on the computer removes all stored access information.
